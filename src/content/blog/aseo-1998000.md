### How does a custom website improve my Google Ads performance?

Custom builds directly lower your customer acquisition costs by improving your Quality Score. Google rewards fast, relevant landing pages with a lower Cost-Per-Click. When your page matches the ad's promise and loads instantly, your conversion rate climbs. You stop wasting budget on bounces and start maximizing the value of every paid click. Better engineering equals better margins on your ad spend.

### What should be the primary goal of an entrepreneur’s website?

The primary goal is generating measurable revenue. Aesthetics and "brand awareness" are secondary to conversion architecture and lead capture. Your site exists to solve a problem for your customers and a growth challenge for your business. If your digital presence isn't actively moving visitors through a sales funnel, it is failing its primary objective. Every pixel must serve your bottom line.
